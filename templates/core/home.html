Phase 2 Foundational Database Models
  • 1. UserProfile (App: accounts)
    Extends Django auth User with 5 distinct roles and phone number.
    OneToOne to User
  • 2. Product (App: masters)
    Finished goods (e.g. Murukku 200g), SKU code, packet size, GST rate (5%), unit price.
    No Overwritten Stock
  • 3. RawMaterial (App: masters)
    Ingredients & packaging (Flour, Oil, Spices, Pouches, Cartons), standard procurement rate.
    UOM: kg, L, pcs
  • 4. Customer & Supplier (App: masters)
    Includes State & GSTIN to calculate Intra-state (CGST+SGST) vs Inter-state (IGST).
    GSTIN Validated
  • 5. BillOfMaterials - BOM (App: masters)
    Batch formulation recipe with unique constraint per Product + Material.
    Batch Size: 200 pkts
Zero Overwritten Balances: In accordance with enterprise ERP standards, Product and RawMaterial do not store manually editable stock balances. Stock will be calculated strictly through immutable ledger entries in Phase 4.
